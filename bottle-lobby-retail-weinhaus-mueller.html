Weinhaus Müller is an independent wine shop in Munich's Glockenbachviertel, family-run since 1998 and known for a hand-picked selection that favours small, authentic producers over supermarket labels.

As a Bottle Lobby retail partner, Weinhaus Müller gets first access to newly onboarded own-label wines before they reach the wider market, and regularly hosts in-store tastings.

Exclusivity Terms

By requesting this partnership, I confirm that any supply, distribution or sale of wines to Weinhaus Müller will take place exclusively through the Bottle Lobby platform and with Bottle Lobby's knowledge and written agreement.

I will not supply Weinhaus Müller through channels outside the platform, nor negotiate separate commercial terms with them without written consent.

I understand that Bottle Lobby will send me a partnership contract to complete and return, and that Weinhaus Müller signs a matching contract on their side.

The partnership only becomes active once both signed contracts have been received and Bottle Lobby confirms it — and breaching these terms may end the partnership and my membership.
