Domaine Lefèvre
📍 Chablis, Burgundy, France · Est. 1962 ✓ VerifiedOwn-Label Partner

Domaine Lefèvre farms Chardonnay on the classic Kimmeridgian limestone slopes above Chablis, where fossilised oyster-shell soils give the wines their signature flinty minerality. The Lefèvre family has tended these Premier Cru parcels for four generations, working with low yields and minimal intervention to let the terroir speak.

Fermentation takes place in a mix of stainless steel and neutral oak, preserving the crisp, precise acidity that defines great Chablis while adding subtle texture. The estate produces a small, carefully allocated volume each vintage — quality and consistency over scale.

As a Bottle Lobby Own-Label Partner, Domaine Lefèvre offers its Premier Cru Chardonnay as a protected, exclusive listing for distributors seeking an authentic, recognisable Burgundy name at an accessible ex-cellar price.
