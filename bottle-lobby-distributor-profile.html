Hawesko GmbH
📍 Hamburg, Germany · Est. 1964 ✓ Verified Own-Label Partner ✦ Wine Show Host

Hawesko GmbH is one of Germany's most respected independent wine distributors, operating from Hamburg and serving over 850 restaurants, hotels, and specialty retailers across northern Germany and Scandinavia since 1964. With deep roots in the Hamburg gastronomy scene, Hawesko has built a reputation for discovering exceptional, under-the-radar producers before they become mainstream.

As a founding Bottle Lobby Distributor Partner, Hawesko co-develops exclusive own-label mono-cépage wines with selected wineries — offering their restaurant clients stable pricing, exclusivity, and consistent supply. Their curated B2B Wine Shows, hosted twice yearly in Hamburg, have become a benchmark event for the northern German wine trade.

Hawesko is actively seeking new winery partnerships for Sauvignon Blanc (Loire), Riesling (Mosel), and premium Primitivo (Sicily) to expand their own-label portfolio heading into 2027.

Active Partnerships
Wineries, restaurants and retailers you have a confirmed, Bottle Lobby-approved partnership with — accepted by both sides, both contracts signed and returned, and confirmed by Bottle Lobby. Only active partners' full profiles and direct contact are open to you, and only active winery partners' wines can appear in your portfolio.
Outgoing Requests
Partnership requests you have sent — to wineries whose wines you want in your portfolio, or to restaurants and retailers you want to supply — and where each one stands. Every partnership needs two signed contracts before Bottle Lobby confirms it.
How a partnership is formed
1. You send a request from a winery's, restaurant's or retailer's public profile and accept the exclusivity terms.
2. They review your request and accept the same terms on their side.
3. Bottle Lobby sends two contracts — one to you, one to them. Both must be signed and returned.
4. Once both signed contracts are with us, Bottle Lobby confirms the partnership and they move to Active Partnerships — full profile and direct contact become available to you.
Incoming Requests
Wineries, restaurants and retailers who have asked to partner with you. Accepting commits both sides to Bottle Lobby's exclusivity terms — we then send both contracts, and confirm the partnership once they are signed and returned.
