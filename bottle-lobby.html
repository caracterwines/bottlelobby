The Problem

The wine trade is fragmented. Everyone loses — until now.

Wineries

No Path to Market

Quality producers with untapped international potential are left without the data, partners, or channels to break into new markets. Trade fairs are expensive and inefficient — and they're disappearing.

Distributors

Too Much Choice, Too Much Risk

Overwhelmed by options and volatile consumer trends, distributors stick to safe, already-listed labels. They lack the tools to discover, validate, and onboard new wines with confidence.

Restaurants

Locked Into the Same List

Restaurants are trapped in their distributor's portfolio — limited variety, price dumping competition, and no flexibility. The wine list next door looks identical to yours.

Retail

No Differentiation, No Margin

Independent retailers carry the same wines as supermarkets and online discounters — making price wars inevitable. Without exclusive labels, in-store events, or direct winery access, standing out is nearly impossible.

More about the Market →

The Solution

One platform. Four pillars. Every stakeholder wins.

A membership-based B2B hub built on four pillars: a live wine guide with smart matchmaking that puts every winery, wine, and distributor in front of the right buyers; curated own-label wines sold under protected, price-stable terms; regional Wine Shows that test demand with real buyers before any volume commitment; and real-time market intelligence, direct ordering tools, and auto-generated materials for every stakeholder. One membership. Four ways to grow.

01

The Wine Guide

The living, interconnected directory of every winery, wine, and distributor. SEO-optimized profiles generate instant visibility — searchable online, and printed twice yearly as a bound reference guide for distributors and buyers.

02

Bottle Lobby Wine Shows

Intimate regional B2B tasting events co-hosted with partner distributors. Wineries present directly to real buyers. Feedback is immediate. Risk is minimal. Placement is faster.

03

Own-Label Wines

Curated, single-varietal wines from their traditional regions — sold under a protected private label. Price-stable, exclusive, and easy to recommend. Replacing existing monocépages without the risk.

04

Intelligence & Services

Real-time market data, trend analytics, and pricing reports for all stakeholders. Auto-generated wine lists, distributor catalogs, branding materials, and import/export support.

How It Works

From discovery to distribution — streamlined.

1

Join as a Member

Wineries and distributors subscribe to the platform. Your profile is indexed, promoted, and matched to the right regional partners based on portfolio gaps and market signals.

2

Enter the Wine Guide & Get Matched

Every wine and profile becomes instantly searchable in the Wine Guide — then our smart matchmaking tool connects wineries with distributors covering 1,000+ restaurants per region, based on real demand data, not guesswork.

3

Present at a Wine Show

Wineries participate in regional B2B tastings hosted with partner distributors. Wines are tested live with real buyers before any large volume commitment is made.

4

List, Sell, and Grow

Confirmed wines are listed under protected own-label branding. Distributors and restaurants benefit from price stability, exclusivity, and consistent supply. Everyone scales together.

Own-Label Strategy

Single varietal. Authentic origin. Protected by design.

Every Bottle Lobby wine is a monocépage — a single grape variety from the region it's historically known for. No blends. No ambiguity. Customers already know what to expect, so restaurants spend less time explaining and more time selling.

Our exclusive own-label model protects all stakeholders from price wars. If a distributor lists our Sauvignon Blanc from Sancerre, no competitor can undercut it with the same bottle.

About the Company

Bottle Lobby was born out of 30 years of hard-won experience importing and marketing products across borders. Founded by Kelly and Serge Khaled — the father-and-son team behind Caracter Media GmbH — it is the platform the wine trade has always needed, built by people who lived its frustrations first-hand.

Kelly Khaled founded Caracter Media in 1987, building decades of expertise across textiles, pharma, and international wine trade. Serge Khaled joined in 2011, bringing a background in advertising and brand strategy from Toronto. Together they identified the structural gap in the B2B wine market — and built Bottle Lobby to fill it. Caracter Media GmbH operates from a 1,600m² logistics facility in Karlsbad, Baden, Germany.
