The Problem

A €300B industry running on broken infrastructure.

The international wine trade is fragmented, opaque, and risk-laden. Every actor in the supply chain suffers — and no one has solved it at scale.

Wineries
01

No Path to Market

Mid-sized producers with quality output and scale can't break into international markets. Trade fairs like PROWEIN cost tens of thousands and rarely convert. They need a data-driven agent, not a booth.

Distributors
02

Overwhelmed & Risk-Averse

Distributors face an oversupply of wine options and volatile consumer trends. Thin margins leave no room for error. They only list wines that are pre-sold — demand must be created for them, not by them.

Restaurants
03

Locked Into Same Lists

Restaurants are trapped in their distributor's portfolio. Price dumping erodes margins on shared labels. They want exclusivity, variety, and consistency — but have no efficient way to get all three.

Our Solution

One platform. Three pillars. Every stakeholder wins.

Bottle Lobby is not just a marketplace — it's a full-stack B2B ecosystem that creates demand, reduces risk, and distributes value across the entire supply chain.

01 / Platform

Digital B2B Hub

Membership-based platform with SEO-optimized winery profiles, smart matchmaking directories, market intelligence tools, direct ordering, and a printed wine guide distributed to regional distributors across Europe.

02 / Product

Own-Label Wines

Curated single-varietal wines from historically authentic regions sold under a protected private label. Price-stable, exclusive, and demand-driven — easy to integrate into existing portfolios without risk.

03 / Events

Bottle Lobby Wine Shows

Regional B2B tasting events co-hosted with partner distributors. Pre-qualified buyers test wines before any large volume commitment. Faster placement, lower risk, real feedback — closing with a pairing dinner.

Why Now?

PROWEIN — the world's largest wine trade fair — is phasing out. The single largest channel for winery-distributor discovery is disappearing, leaving a critical gap in the market that a digital-first platform is uniquely positioned to fill.

Post-pandemic volatility has accelerated the shift toward mono-cépage wines — simple, predictable varieties from known regions. This trend plays directly into our portfolio strategy and simplifies placement across all stakeholders.

The timing for a lean, data-powered B2B wine platform has never been better. The pain is acute, the incumbents are absent, and the technology is ready.

Business Model

Six revenue streams. All aligned with value.

Our model is designed for scalability and long-term partner alignment. We take a small margin to drive volume — making us highly attractive to distributors while building sustainable, recurring revenue.

◎

Membership Fees

Annual or quarterly subscriptions from wineries and distributors for platform access, trend analytics, matchmaking, and profile indexing.

Recurring · Core Revenue
◈

Complementary Services

Premium add-ons: branding & label design, PR & press campaigns, import compliance, market research reports, virtual tastings, and wine list consulting.

Project-Based · High Margin
✦

Wine Show Fees

Wineries pay participation fees to join regional B2B tastings hosted with partner distributors — covering planning, promotion, and buyer access.

Event-Based · Scalable
◇

Commission on Sales

A small per-bottle margin on transactions facilitated through our distribution network. Low margin, high volume — aligned with partner success.

Volume-Based · Long-Term
⬡

Exclusivity Transfer Fee

When Bottle Lobby secures market placement for a winery and an importer requests exclusivity, a one-time success fee is charged plus an ongoing commission per bottle.

Success-Based · High Value
◐

Data Access Packages

Advanced trend analytics, consumer behavior reports, and regional demand forecasts offered to distributors and retail partners as premium data subscriptions.

SaaS-Style · Future Stream
