SICILY
CR
Cantina Rossi
📍 Sicily, Italy · Est. 1987 ✓ Verified Own-Label Partner ✦ Wine Show Member

Cantina Rossi is a family-owned estate nestled in the sun-drenched hills of western Sicily, producing expressive, terroir-driven Primitivo and Nero d'Avola since 1987. Under the stewardship of the Rossi family for three generations, the estate has grown from 12 to over 80 hectares of certified vineyards at 400–550 metres altitude.

With an annual production capacity of over 1.2 million bottles, Cantina Rossi is large enough to guarantee supply consistency while maintaining the craftsmanship and origin authenticity that international buyers demand. Their Primitivo — sourced from 40-year-old bush vines — is a benchmark expression of the variety from Sicily's most celebrated sub-zones.

As a Bottle Lobby Own-Label Partner, Cantina Rossi offers distributors a protected, demand-driven portfolio of single-varietal wines designed for easy listing, stable pricing, and long-term exclusivity.

HAMBURG
HW
Hawesko GmbH
📍 Hamburg, Germany · Est. 1964 ✓ Verified Own-Label Partner ✦ Wine Show Host

Hawesko GmbH is one of Germany's most respected independent wine distributors, operating from Hamburg and serving over 850 restaurants, hotels, and specialty retailers across northern Germany and Scandinavia since 1964. With deep roots in the Hamburg gastronomy scene, Hawesko has built a reputation for discovering exceptional, under-the-radar producers before they become mainstream.

As a founding Bottle Lobby Distributor Partner, Hawesko co-develops exclusive own-label mono-cépage wines with selected wineries — offering their restaurant clients stable pricing, exclusivity, and consistent supply. Their curated B2B Wine Shows, hosted twice yearly in Hamburg, have become a benchmark event for the northern German wine trade.

Hawesko is actively seeking new winery partnerships for Sauvignon Blanc (Loire), Riesling (Mosel), and premium Primitivo (Sicily) to expand their own-label portfolio heading into 2027.
