Bistro Laurent
📍 Frankfurt, Germany · Est. 2015 ✓ Verified Own-Label Partner ✦ Wine Show Guest

Bistro Laurent is a modern European bistro in Frankfurt's Westend district, known for a sommelier-curated wine list that favours small, authentic producers over mass-market labels. Since 2015, Head Sommelier Sophie Laurent has built a 14-label wine program where more than half the bottles are exclusive to the restaurant — sourced through Bottle Lobby's own-label network for genuine scarcity and price protection.

As a Bottle Lobby restaurant partner, Bistro Laurent gets first access to newly onboarded own-label wines before they reach the broader market, plus direct lines to distributors and, where relevant, the wineries themselves — a shortcut past the usual layers of the trade.

Currently expanding the by-the-glass program and looking to add more indigenous Mediterranean varieties to the list heading into 2027.

Active Distributors
Distributors you have a confirmed, Bottle Lobby-approved partnership with. Only a distributor's full profile, direct contact and their wine portfolio are open to you — restaurants source exclusively through distributors, never directly from a winery.
Distributor Requests
Partnership requests you have sent to distributors, and where each one stands. Every partnership needs two signed contracts — yours and the distributor's — before Bottle Lobby confirms it.
How a partnership is formed
1. You send a request from the distributor's public profile — the right one is shown on every wine's article page under "View Distributors" — and accept the exclusivity terms.
2. The distributor reviews your request and accepts the same terms on their side.
3. Bottle Lobby sends two contracts — one to you, one to the distributor. Both must be signed and returned.
4. Once both signed contracts are with us, Bottle Lobby confirms the partnership and the distributor moves to Active Distributors — full profile, direct contact and their wine portfolio become available to you.
Incoming Requests
Distributors who have asked to partner with you. Accepting commits both sides to Bottle Lobby's exclusivity terms — we then send both contracts, and confirm the partnership once they are signed and returned.
