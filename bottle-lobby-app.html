For Every Stakeholder

One app. Every role.

The Bottle Lobby App is built differently for each type of member. Same platform — tailored experience depending on whether you're a winery, distributor, restaurant, or retailer.

For Wineries

Stay connected to your buyers, monitor orders, share technical documentation, and coordinate events — all from your phone.

📊

Live Order Monitoring

See every order placed for your wines in real time — quantities, distributors, regions, and reorder patterns. No more guessing what's selling where.

💬

Direct Chat with Buyers

Communicate directly with sommeliers, restaurant buyers, and distributors. Answer questions about your wines, share tasting notes, and build genuine relationships.

📄

Share Technical Sheets

Upload and share Technical Sheets and Wine Expertises directly from the app. Buyers can download them instantly — no email chains required.

✦

Event Planning & Group Chat

Coordinate B2B Wine Shows with distributors and restaurants directly in the app. Group chats, shared documents, schedules, and event materials — all in one place.

🔔

Promotion Notifications

Push notifications when your wines are added to a restaurant's wine list, when a new distributor connects with you, or when a buyer places a large order.

For Distributors

Manage your portfolio, create promotions, plan events, and stay connected to your winery partners and restaurant clients — on the go.

🎁

Create & Push Promotions

Set up deals like "60 + 6 free" or "free shipping above 60 bottles" and push them directly to all your restaurant and retail clients via app notification. Instant demand creation.

📦

Order & Delivery Management

Track all incoming orders from restaurants and retailers. Monitor dispatch and delivery status in real time. Set estimated arrival dates visible to clients.

✦

Co-plan Wine Shows

Create event group chats with wineries and restaurant clients. Share event schedules, wine lists, and logistics documents directly in the app. Everyone stays on the same page.

💬

Client Communication Hub

Chat with all your restaurant and retail clients, and with your winery partners — in one place. No more WhatsApp chaos. Professional, documented, searchable.

📊

Portfolio & Volume Overview

See your complete own-label portfolio performance at a glance — volumes per wine, per region, per client. Spot trends before they become problems.

For Restaurants

Reorder in seconds, generate your wine card instantly, create table tents, and chat directly with the winemaker — all from your phone.

🛒

One-Tap Reorder

See all wines in your current list. When stock is running low, tap to reorder with the same supplier — quantity, delivery date, done. In under 30 seconds.

📋

Auto-Generate Wine List

Select your wines from your Bottle Lobby portfolio, enter your prices, and export a professional, print-ready wine card — with descriptions and origins pulled automatically from winery profiles. Perfect for seasonal menu changes.

🪧

Table Tent Creator

Choose DIN A6 or DIN A7 format, select your wines, and generate a print-ready table tent for your restaurant. Slide it into the stand — done. Update it whenever your list changes.

🔔

Deal & Promotion Alerts

Get instant notifications when your distributor runs a special offer — 60 + 6 free, free delivery thresholds, limited seasonal wines. Never miss a deal again.

💬

Chat with the Winemaker

Your sommelier can chat directly with the producer — ask about vintages, food pairings, harvest conditions. Those stories sell bottles. Now you have them at your fingertips.

For Retail

Order flexibly, create professional brochures and event materials, track deliveries, and access every technical sheet — all in one app.

📦

Just-in-Time Ordering

Order exactly what you need for an upcoming event or campaign — no large volume commitments. The app shows stock availability and estimated delivery dates in real time.

📰

Mini Brochure Generator

Select wines from your portfolio, add retail prices, and export a professional mini brochure — perfect for in-store placement, events, or customer handouts. All wine info pulled automatically from winery profiles.

📄

Technical Sheets on Demand

Download Technical Sheets and Wine Expertises for any wine in your portfolio — instantly, from the winery's profile. Perfect for training staff or responding to customer questions.

🔔

New Deals & Arrival Alerts

Be notified when a new exclusive wine is available in your region, when a special offer is activated, or when you haven't ordered in a while — so you never run out.

🎪

Event Material Creator

Planning an in-store tasting or winemaker evening? Generate event invitations, wine presentation sheets, and tasting notes — all branded and print-ready. Share digitally or print on the day.
