My Press & Recognition
Magazine features and wine-guide mentions. Once published, they appear on your public profile automatically.
My Distributors
Confirmed partnerships, grouped by region. These distributors see your full profile and can list your wines. Everyone else only sees your public summary until a partnership is confirmed.
My Requests
Incoming Requests
Distributors who want to work with you. Accepting a request commits both sides to Bottle Lobby's exclusivity terms — we then send you the contract, and confirm the partnership once it is signed.
Outgoing Requests
Partnership requests you have sent to distributors, and where each one stands. Every partnership needs two signed contracts — yours and the distributor's — before Bottle Lobby confirms it.
How a partnership is formed
1. You send a request from the distributor's public profile and accept the exclusivity terms.
2. The distributor reviews your request and accepts the same terms on their side.
3. Bottle Lobby sends two contracts — one to you, one to the distributor. Both must be signed and returned.
4. Once both signed contracts are with us, Bottle Lobby confirms the partnership and the distributor moves to My Distributors.
My Stars
Distributors, restaurants and retailers you follow — a way to bookmark accounts you like, want to remember, or are considering for a future partnership.
My Fans
Restaurants and retailers who follow your winery. Anyone who already lists your wines through a distributor partner can request a tasting directly — this list is also visible to your distributor partners under their own "Opportunities" section.

Active Partnerships
Wineries, restaurants and retailers you have a confirmed, Bottle Lobby-approved partnership with. Only active partners' full profiles and direct contact are open to you, and only active winery partners' wines can appear in your Wine Portfolio.
Requests
Incoming Requests
Wineries, restaurants and retailers who have asked to partner with you. Accepting commits both sides to Bottle Lobby's exclusivity terms.
Outgoing Requests
Partnership requests you have sent — to wineries whose wines you want in your portfolio, or to restaurants and retailers you want to supply — and where each one stands.
How a partnership is formed
1. You send a request from a winery's, restaurant's or retailer's public profile and accept the exclusivity terms.
2. They review your request and accept the same terms on their side.
3. Bottle Lobby sends two contracts — one to you, one to them. Both must be signed and returned.
4. Once both signed contracts are with us, Bottle Lobby confirms the partnership and they move to Active Partnerships.
My Promo Materials
Branded merchandise and marketing materials you offer your restaurant and retail partners — glasses, tasting stands, menu cards and more — unlocked once a customer hits a condition you set. All active partners see these under their own "Promo Materials" and get notified the moment you publish a new one.
Exclusive Offers
Single-wine offers for your restaurant and retail partners — a discount or a free sample pack, typically used to introduce a new arrival or move a special-tier wine. Visible under "Exclusive Offers" on every active partner's dashboard.
Exclusive Deals
Quantity-driven deals — always placed as a single order, never accumulated over time. Reward a percentage discount, or free goods on one wine or a mixed case.
My Opportunities
Wineries your own restaurant and retail partners are following. If you already carry the winery, that's a ready-made tasting. If multiple partners follow the same one, that's a Wine Show waiting to happen. If it's new to you, it's a candidate for your own portfolio.
My Stars
Wineries, restaurants and retailers you follow — a way to bookmark producers and accounts you like, want to remember, or are considering for a future partnership.
My Fans
Wineries, restaurants and retailers who follow you — a signal of interest you can use to reach out and start a conversation.
