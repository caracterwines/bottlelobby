One System, Not Four Silos

Connecting the dots.

The four pillars aren't separate products bolted together — they share one live database. Add a wine, host a show, strike an own-label deal, or track a trend, and it updates everywhere else automatically. No re-entering the same information twice.

A winery lists a Primitivo → it's instantly searchable in the Wine Guide → a distributor discovers it through Matchmaking → they present it together at a Wine Show → it becomes an exclusive Own-Label deal → Intelligence & Services tracks its performance at every restaurant that lists it.
One entry. Five outcomes.
