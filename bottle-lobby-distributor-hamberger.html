Hamberger is a long-established Munich beverage cash & carry wholesaler, serving Bavarian gastronomy since 1968 with a full range spanning beer, soft drinks, spirits and wine, from a large-format warehouse just outside the city.

As a Bottle Lobby Distributor Partner, Hamberger is growing a curated still-wine selection alongside its traditional cash & carry range, starting with a Burgundy Chardonnay sourced through the platform.

The team is scouting further French and Austrian wineries to build out a compact, well-chosen list for its Bavarian restaurant and hotel customers.
