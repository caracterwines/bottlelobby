About Primitivo

Character

Deep-coloured, high-alcohol, and richly fruited — think blackberry, plum and warm spice. Ranges from juicy and approachable at entry level to structured and age-worthy in riserva bottlings.

Where It Grows

Best known from Puglia's Salento peninsula and Sicily's warmer western zones, typically on old bush vines (alberello) that naturally limit yields and concentrate flavour.
