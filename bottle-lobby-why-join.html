Real Case Study

How we brought Adyar to Germany.

This is what a Bottle Lobby market entry strategy looks like in practice. We executed this full campaign for Adyar — Lebanon's oldest monastic winery — and it's exactly what we do for every winery member who needs to enter a new market.

● Live Case Study · Caracter Media × Adyar
ADYAR

Oldest Monastic Winery of Lebanon · Crusader Era Heritage

Adyar is one of Lebanon's most extraordinary wine estates — nine monasteries, centuries of history, and the potential to equal Lebanon's entire current wine output. The challenge: zero presence in Germany, no distributor network, no market visibility.

Bottle Lobby was asked to change that. Here's exactly what we did.

The brief was simple but ambitious: take a winery with no German presence and no existing distribution relationships, and create real, lasting demand in premium gastronomy — within months, not years.

Our approach combined press strategy, distributor federation integration, a live B2B event series, and full import coordination — all synchronized so that by the time the press coverage launched, the product was already in Germany and ready to deliver.

The result was a complete, risk-free market entry that gave Adyar immediate placement in premium restaurants and a foundation for long-term national expansion.

The 4-Phase Strategy

1
The Lebanon Trip

We curated an exclusive trip to Lebanon for journalists from Meininger, Falstaff, Vinum, and Der Feinschmecker — plus senior representatives from the FFD, Germany's national federation of leading wine wholesalers. They visited all nine Adyar monasteries, met the monks, and experienced the winery's spiritual heritage first-hand.

2
Press & PR Launch

Journalists returned as long-term ambassadors — publishing reviews, monastery stories, and brand features across Germany's top wine media. Coverage in Meininger, Falstaff, and Der Feinschmecker created national visibility and positioned Adyar as a cultural discovery, not just another imported wine.

3
FLAIR of ADYAR Events

We hosted a series of high-impact regional B2B events — "FLAIR of ADYAR" — in collaboration with FFD member distributors across Germany. Each event featured Adyar's oenologist, a Maronite monk, as the living embodiment of the brand. Distributors invited their key gastronomy clients. Orders were placed on the night.

4
Import & Availability

Caracter Media secured the full German import in advance — so that the moment press coverage launched and events created demand, the product was already in Germany and ready to deliver. Zero risk for distributors. Immediate fulfillment for restaurants.
