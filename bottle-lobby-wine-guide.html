The Wine Guide

Every wine, winery, and distributor — in one place.

The live directory of everything on Bottle Lobby. Every filter below is pulled straight from what wineries and distributors enter into their own profiles — add a wine, and it's instantly searchable here too.

✦ Already a member? We also produce a printed edition of the Wine Guide each quarter — the same directory, laid out for tastings, trade shows, and client meetings. Members can request a hard copy at no cost.

✦ How this works: when a winery adds or edits a wine in their Wine Portfolio, it updates here automatically — the filters below are computed live from that same data, not a separate list.
✦ How this works: a winery's country, region, and grape varieties here are set once on their own profile — they're never re-entered anywhere else.
✦ How this works: a distributor's winery partners and own-label count are drawn from the wines they've actually pulled into their own Wine Portfolio — nothing is entered twice.
✦ How this works: a restaurant's wine list is pulled from the wines their distributor partner already carries — nothing is entered twice. Note: in the live platform, restaurant profiles are only visible here to paying Bottle Lobby members — shown to everyone for now while this directory is in preview.
✦ How this works: a retailer's wine selection is pulled from the wines their distributor partner already carries — nothing is entered twice. Note: in the live platform, retailer profiles are only visible here to paying Bottle Lobby members — shown to everyone for now while this directory is in preview.
✦ How this works: one directory, two kinds of record. Wine Shows are the Bottle Lobby format — reviewed and released by Bottle Lobby before anything is promised. Member Events are members' own occasions, published by their hosts; Bottle Lobby neither reviews nor releases them and gives no guarantee for them. Both render live from the same records the member dashboards read — nothing is copied onto this page.
