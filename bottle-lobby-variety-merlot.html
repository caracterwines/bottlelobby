About Merlot

Character

Soft and plummy, with black cherry, vanilla and cocoa notes. Generally lower in tannin and more approachable young than Cabernet Sauvignon.

Where It Grows

One of the world's most planted red grapes. This bottling comes from Bordeaux's right-bank-influenced clay-limestone soils, where Merlot has long been the dominant variety.
