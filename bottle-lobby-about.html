About Bottle Lobby

Built by people who
know wine — and know
its broken trade.

Bottle Lobby was born out of 30 years of hard-won experience importing and marketing products across borders. Founded by Kelly and Serge Khaled — the father-and-son team behind Caracter Media GmbH — it is the platform the wine trade has always needed, built by people who lived its frustrations first-hand.

The Origin

A problem we lived every day.

Bottle Lobby didn't start in a boardroom. It started in a warehouse in Karlsbad — and in decades of experience importing goods across borders. Kelly Khaled founded Caracter Media's parent company in 1987, and over the following 30 years built a deep understanding of what it truly takes to bring a product to the German market.

When Serge Khaled returned from six years studying advertising and marketing in Toronto in 2011, he brought a new layer to the family operation — a marketing department, a data-driven mindset, and a clear diagnosis of the wine trade's deepest problem: not quality, not supply, but infrastructure. Wineries couldn't reach buyers. Distributors couldn't find the right wines. Restaurants were locked into homogeneous lists.

The industry's answer was always the same: go to PROWEIN. Spend €30,000 on a booth, hope someone discovers you among 60,000 wines, and repeat next year. With events like PROWEIN now phasing out, even that broken solution was disappearing.

"We kept receiving calls from wineries around the world asking how to enter Germany. The story was always identical: there's no functioning channel. So we built one."

— Serge Khaled, Co-Founder, Bottle Lobby

Bottle Lobby is the result of that frustration — and years of industry experience translated into a platform, a product strategy, and a network that actually works for every actor in the chain.

Key Milestones

1987

Kelly Khaled Founds the Company

Kelly Khaled establishes the parent company in Germany — initially as a textile import and fashion retail business. Stable growth soon enables expansion into promotional marketing for pharmaceutical and cosmetic industry clients.

2011

Serge Khaled Returns from Canada

After 6 years studying advertising and marketing in Toronto, Serge joins the family business — integrating a fully operating advertisement department and bringing a data-driven marketing perspective to the company's operations.

2019

The Problem is Recognised

After years in the wine trade, the Khaled family identifies the systemic inefficiencies breaking the supply chain — wineries with no path to market, distributors drowning in choice, restaurants locked into homogeneous lists. The decision is made: this problem needs a platform.

2023

Concept Development

The Bottle Lobby concept takes shape — a membership-based B2B platform combining market intelligence, matchmaking, own-label wine strategy, and regional wine shows into a single ecosystem.

2025

Business Plan & Pilot Preparation

The full business plan is developed. First distributor conversations begin in Munich and Hamburg. The own-label wine strategy is validated with three regional pilot distributors.

2026

Bottle Lobby Launches

Platform goes live. First 15 winery members onboarded. First 3 Wine Shows confirmed in Munich and Hamburg. Seed funding round open at €150,000.

What We Stand For

Six principles that guide everything we do.

◎
01

Transparency

Every stakeholder in the wine trade deserves clear, honest information — about pricing, trends, demand, and relationships. We build transparency into every interaction on our platform.

◈
02

Authenticity

We only work with wines that are genuinely from where they claim to be. Our own-label strategy is built on historically authentic regions — because provenance is not a marketing claim, it's the foundation of trust.

◇
03

Efficiency

The wine trade is full of waste — wasted trips, wasted booths, wasted marketing spend. We are obsessively focused on removing friction at every step of the supply chain.

✦
04

Fairness

A platform is only as good as the value it creates for each party. We design every feature, pricing model, and partnership to ensure wineries, distributors, and restaurants all benefit equitably.

⬡
05

Data over Guesswork

Too many wine decisions are made on gut feeling. We believe in market intelligence — real consumer data, trend analytics, and regional demand signals that make every decision smarter and lower-risk.

◐
06

Long-term Thinking

We take a small margin to drive volume and loyalty. We are not interested in quick wins. We are building relationships and infrastructure that will still matter in twenty years.

The Parent Company

Backed by decades of real experience.

Bottle Lobby is built on the foundation of Caracter Media GmbH — a German trade and marketing cooperation founded by Kelly Khaled in 1987. What began as a textile import and fashion retail company grew steadily into a diversified operation spanning consumer goods, promotional marketing for companies like Hoffmann La Roche, and cosmetic packaging for brands such as L'Oréal.

The company expanded its infrastructure with a 1,600 m² warehouse and extended into a broad range of consumer goods specifically targeted at the German market. When Serge Khaled returned from his advertising studies in Toronto in 2011, a fully operating advertisement department was integrated — completing the expertise needed to build Bottle Lobby.

30 years of importing products from all over the world taught the founding family one thing above all: branding, marketing and distribution strategy are the difference between a great product succeeding — or disappearing — in a new market. That hard-won knowledge is the backbone of everything Bottle Lobby does.
