Cantina Rossi
📍 Sicily, Italy · Est. 1987 ✓ Verified Own-Label Partner ✦ Wine Show Member

Cantina Rossi is a family-owned estate nestled in the sun-drenched hills of western Sicily, producing expressive, terroir-driven Primitivo and Nero d'Avola since 1987. Under the stewardship of the Rossi family for three generations, the estate has grown from 12 to over 80 hectares of certified vineyards at 400–550 metres altitude.

With an annual production capacity of over 1.2 million bottles, Cantina Rossi is large enough to guarantee supply consistency while maintaining the craftsmanship and origin authenticity that international buyers demand. Their Primitivo — sourced from 40-year-old bush vines — is a benchmark expression of the variety from Sicily's most celebrated sub-zones.

As a Bottle Lobby Own-Label Partner, Cantina Rossi offers distributors a protected, demand-driven portfolio of single-varietal wines designed for easy listing, stable pricing, and long-term exclusivity.

Partnership requests you have sent to distributors, and where each one stands. Every partnership needs two signed contracts — yours and the distributor's — before Bottle Lobby confirms it.
How a partnership is formed
1. You send a request from the distributor's public profile and accept the exclusivity terms.
2. The distributor reviews your request and accepts the same terms on their side.
3. Bottle Lobby sends two contracts — one to you, one to the distributor. Both must be signed and returned.
4. Once both signed contracts are with us, Bottle Lobby confirms the partnership and the distributor moves to Active Distributors — full profile, direct contact and your wines become available to them.
Incoming Requests
Distributors who want to work with you — restaurants and retailers now source exclusively through a distributor, so partnership requests only come from distributors. Accepting a request commits both sides to Bottle Lobby's exclusivity terms — we then send you the contract, and confirm the partnership once it is signed.
